# Flipit — Product Overview

Flipit is a third-person isometric RPG built in Unity 6 where a child protagonist navigates a procedurally generated city from school to home.

## Core Gameplay

- **Exploration**: Player traverses a grid-based city with streets, sidewalks, buildings, parks, and landmarks
- **Dialogue**: Proximity-based NPC interactions with branching conversations, typewriter text effect, and event-driven option filtering
- **Combat**: Both voluntary encounters (Challenger NPCs) and forced encounters (hidden trigger zones) that transition to a dedicated combat scene
- **Vendors**: Commercial NPCs that sell items to the player

## Setting & Theme

- Spanish-themed (NPC names, dialogue content, and some documentation are in Spanish)
- Urban environment with placeholder geometry (cubes, cylinders, planes) for prototyping
- Isometric camera perspective with building transparency when occluding the player

## Key Landmarks

- **School**: Player spawn point and route start
- **House**: Player destination and route end
- NPCs: Vendors, Challengers, Guards, Collectors, Shopkeepers
